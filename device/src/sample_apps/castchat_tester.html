Castchat Test Tool

Basic usage

  • Fill out the base URL, app name, namespace, and user-agent fields
  • Press Connect to open the websocket connection. Disconnect to close.
  • Press Send Hello to send the handshake and bind to the app with user-agent information.
  • YOU MUST SEND HELLO WITHIN FIVE SECONDS UPON CONNECT
  • Send Message will send the contents of the message field
  • Select a message on the left of the messages textarea to automatically populate it.
